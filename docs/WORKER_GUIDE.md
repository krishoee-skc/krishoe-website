# KRISHOE Worker Portal Guide

## Access Your Portal

### Mobile (Recommended)
1. Open your phone browser
2. Go to `https://krishoe-website.vercel.app`
3. Look for "Install app" button
4. Click "Install" (works like an app)
5. App opens full screen

### Web
1. Go to `https://krishoe-website.vercel.app/worker/dashboard`
2. Enter your Worker ID when prompted
3. Your dashboard loads

## Understanding Your Earnings

### Components:

**Base Salary**: Fixed monthly amount (if applicable)

**Piece Rate**: Rs. per pair you complete
- Formula: Pairs × Rate = Earnings
- Rate varies by product & category

**Attendance Bonus**: Extra Rs. if attendance good

**Quality Bonus**: 5% extra if:
- Quality rate > 95% AND
- Attendance > 90%

**Deductions**: If any (advances, etc.)

**Net Pay**: What you actually get paid

### Example:
```
Piece Rate Earnings:      Rs. 25,000
Attendance Bonus:         Rs.    500
Quality Bonus (eligible): Rs.  1,250
Deductions:              Rs.   (250)
─────────────────────────────────────
NET PAY:                 Rs. 26,500 ✅
```

### Monthly Payslip

- Your name & ID
- Month & year
- Detailed earnings breakdown
- Deductions
- Net pay
- Status (Paid/Approved/Pending)

## Bonus Eligibility

### Bonus Calculation:

**5% bonus on your monthly earnings if:**

1. ✅ Quality Rate ≥ 95%
2. ✅ Attendance Rate ≥ 90%

**Both conditions must be true.**

### Example:

Monthly earnings: Rs. 25,000
Quality rate: 97% ✅
Attendance: 92% ✅

Bonus = Rs. 25,000 × 5% = **Rs. 1,250** 🎁

### Tracking Bonus:

1. Check **📊 Overview** tab
2. Look at quality & attendance
3. See if bonus eligible
4. Bonus shown in payslip

## FAQ

**Q: How often do I get paid?**
A: Monthly. Payroll processed every month.

**Q: When will my payslip be ready?**
A: End of month. Check portal after 1st.

**Q: How is piece rate calculated?**
A: Pairs × Rate per pair = Your earnings

**Q: What if I was absent?**
A: Attendance % drops. Bonus not eligible.

**Q: Can I download my payslip?**
A: Yes! Click earnings tab → Download PDF

**Q: How do I improve my quality?**
A: Focus on work, check each pair, report issues

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes. Only you can see your info with your ID.

**Q: The app is slow/has bugs.**
A: Check internet. Report to admin if issues persist.
